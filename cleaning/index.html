Questions people ask

Do I need to be home?

No. Many clients leave a key or a door code. We store access details securely and never label keys with your address.

What if something gets damaged?

We're fully insured. Tell us within 48 hours and we'll repair or replace the item.

Can I change or cancel a visit?

Yes, free of charge with 24 hours' notice. Later cancellations are charged 50% of the visit price.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, including a vacuum and microfibre cloths. Ask for our fragrance-free, eco-friendly kit at no extra cost.
