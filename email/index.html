Email marketing case study, concept project

Turning new subscribers into first-time buyers

For Nsaa Textiles, a fictional online fabric shop, I planned and designed two Mailchimp automations: a three-email welcome series for new subscribers and an abandoned-cart email for shoppers who leave before paying.

The goal

Most people who join a shop's email list don't buy straight away, and many shoppers abandon their cart before paying. Two automations address both moments, running in the background without manual work.

Who it's for

  • New subscribers who signed up on the website
  • Shoppers who added items to their cart but didn't check out
  • Mostly Canadians who love African textiles, sewists and gift buyers

What success looks like

  • More subscribers make a first purchase within 7 days
  • Abandoned carts recovered without discounting
  • Low unsubscribe rate, because emails are useful, not pushy

Why no discount here?

Offering a discount every time trains shoppers to abandon carts on purpose. Instead, the email reminds them their order already ships free and invites questions, which answers the most common reasons people hesitate.

The four emails

Each email is hand-coded in HTML that works in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail, adapts to phones, uses real text instead of text inside images, and includes alt text, a preheader, and an unsubscribe link.

How it's set up in Mailchimp

Audience and data

  • One audience with a signup form on the website
  • Merge field for first name, used as *|FNAME|* to personalise greetings
  • Tags: "Welcome series" and "Customer"
  • Interest groups for Fabric, Scarves, Bags and Home, used for future targeted campaigns

Consent and accessibility

  • Signup form asks for clear permission to email, as Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) requires
  • Every email names the sender and includes an unsubscribe link
  • Text at 14px or larger, strong colour contrast, alt text on every image
  • Key information in live text, so it shows even when images are blocked

How I'd measure results

These are the numbers I would track in Mailchimp reports after launch, and what each one tells the business.
