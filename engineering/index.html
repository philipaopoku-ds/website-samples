What we do

Four focused services, delivered by engineers who have worked on site. You get one team from the first study to the final as-built drawings.

Process design

Heat and material balances, equipment sizing and P&IDs for new builds and debottlenecking.

  • Process simulation
  • P&IDs
  • Equipment sizing

Pipeline integrity

Inspection planning, risk assessments and repair recommendations that keep lines safe and compliant.

  • Risk assessment
  • Inspection plans
  • Fitness for service

  • Scope definition

How a project runs

Every project follows the same four stages, so you always know what happens next and who is responsible.

  1. Site visit

    We walk the facility with your operators and agree the problem we are solving.

  2. Proposal

    A fixed scope, schedule and price within five business days.

  3. Engineering

    Weekly updates, with drawings and calculations shared as they are ready.

  4. Handover

    Stamped deliverables, as-built drawings and a walkthrough for your team.
